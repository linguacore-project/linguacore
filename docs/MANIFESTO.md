# LinguaCore Manifesto

1. Knowledge before software.
2. Evidence before interpretation.
3. Communities are partners, not data sources.
4. Knowledge is referenced, not duplicated.
5. Every persistent object is versioned and addressable.
6. Every scientific assertion has provenance.
7. External standards are adapters, not the internal ontology.
8. Automated systems may suggest; accountable humans validate and publish.
9. Access restrictions and community governance are part of the data model.
10. Data must outlive the present implementation.
