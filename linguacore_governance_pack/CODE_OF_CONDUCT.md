# Code of Conduct

LinguaCore is a scientific and community-oriented project. Participation requires professional, respectful, and responsible conduct.

## Expected conduct

Participants should:

- discuss ideas and code without attacking people;
- respect different levels of technical, linguistic, and community expertise;
- acknowledge sources, collaborators, and community knowledge holders;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and unwanted personal attention;
- handle culturally sensitive knowledge and community restrictions with care;
- accept correction and resolve disagreement through evidence and documented reasoning.

## Unacceptable conduct

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, deliberate misrepresentation, disclosure of private or restricted information, plagiarism, threats, and sustained disruption of project work.

## Reporting

Until a dedicated private reporting address is established, reports should be sent privately to the organization owner through their GitHub profile rather than posted in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and proportionately.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repositories, issue trackers, reviews, discussions, project events, and other spaces where a participant represents LinguaCore. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issue warnings, restrict participation, or ban accounts when necessary to protect the project and its participants.
